User story method

Design around the dispatcher’s goal—not around a feature list

As a heavy-transport dispatcher, I want to check the exact permitted route against current and trustworthy road conditions so that I can decide whether the transport can depart without becoming stranded.
PermitRoute geometry, ID, and time window
TransportWeight, width, height, and length
ConditionsClosures, roadworks, and incidents
EvidenceSource, freshness, and confidence
DecisionReady, Monitor, or Hold
Critical boundaryThe tool supports a dispatch decision. It does not issue the permit or calculate an unapproved detour.

Research honesty

What we know—and what the prototype still assumes

Supported by current research

  • Heavy transports depend on fixed, approved routes.
  • Short-notice changes can create costly delays.
  • Roadworks information can be outdated or incomplete.
  • Dispatchers need earlier route-status information.

Still assumed

  • A useful VEMAGS route can be imported as JSON.
  • Participants will provide timely, trustworthy observations.
  • MDS can combine all required data sources.
  • Ready, Monitor, and Hold fit the dispatcher’s mental model.
Not yet doneNo usability test or Wizard of Oz session has been conducted. Authorities and hearing bodies have not yet been interviewed.
Do not present prototype maturity as validation12
